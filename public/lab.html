Frame Delivery

How frames actually reach the screen. Composed Flip = the compositor is adding ~1 frame of latency (borderless done wrong). With Reflex titles, also shows measured click-to-photon input latency. Run while a game or video is on screen.

GPU Thieves

Per-process 3D-engine usage — who is rendering besides your game. Wallpaper engines, overlay replays, and forgotten browser tabs show up here. Takes ~2s.

Interruptions

Focus-steal forensics: every window that took the foreground during a competitive session, with its process. Eco-cage a repeat offender to throttle it in the background without killing it.

Refresh-Rate Guard

Panels silently fall back to 60 Hz after driver updates and cable re-plugs. Checks every display's current Hz against its panel maximum at the current resolution.

VRAM Headroom

VRAM eviction is stutter. Names the processes holding dedicated VRAM and how much headroom the 12 GB card has left.

Install Locality

Benchmarks each drive's real read speed and maps which titles live where — a heavy title on the slowest drive costs load time every day. Takes up to a minute.

Interrupt Shepherding

Steers NIC interrupts to the top CPU cores, away from core 0's kernel DPC traffic and the cores your game's threads favor. GPU stays on MSI-X defaults deliberately. Apply restarts the adapter (~2s blip) and is refused while a game is live. Fully revertible.
